Why they are separate

The shared parser treats dates as month-first. For tenants whose locale writes day first, that can reverse the day and month. This is the parser cause.

The hint cause is limited to booking-v3 forms in day-first tenants. The form tells people to enter MM/DD/YYYY, while the tenant's date handling reads day first. The form hint creates a different failure from the shared parser.

The tenant locale and booking form determine the cause. The intake tag is only a guess and disagrees with that classification on 38 unique reports. The desk noted five reports as one-day moves; all five remain in the parser group under the tenant and form rules.
